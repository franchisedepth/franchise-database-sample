## Fee notes
2nd territory $7,500; larger territories at $1/home over 50,000. Non-refundable. Minimum local marketing spend of $12,500 for a single unit territory during the first full Peak Season. From the second calendar year of operation, the franchisee must spend annually the greater of 5% of Gross Sales or $15,000 on local advertising. Advertising Cooperative contributions (currently $0; up to $25,000 per calendar year) are credited toward the local advertising expenditure.
