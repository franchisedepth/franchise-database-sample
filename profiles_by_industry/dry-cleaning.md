## Fee notes
Plant Store: $30,000. Satellite Store: $12,500 standard, $6,000 if signed in 2024 and opened by Dec 31, 2024 (Satellite Store Incentive Program). Qualified veterans: $0. Legacy Plant Store Renewal: $0. Development Rights Agreement: $5,000 per store (credited toward franchise fee). New Plant Stores must offer pickup and delivery (PUD) Services under the PUD Addendum. It adds a Technology Fee of 5% of Gross PUD Revenues, currently paid to affiliate COW. It also adds Marketing Fund contributions of 3.5% (Customer Acquisition) and 2% (Customer Development and Retention) of Gross PUD Revenues. The royalty and 3% Marketing Fund also apply to PUD revenue.
