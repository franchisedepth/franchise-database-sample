## Fee notes
Standard fee $42,000. Multi-unit discount to $27,000 for 2nd+ units. Veteran discount to $31,500. Transfer fee $10,000 (IFF waived). Renewal fee $8,000 (IFF waived). MicroSchool amendment initial fee $10,000. During 2025, charged between $0 and $36,000. Advertising minimum: you must spend at least $57,000 per year on Huntington Services and eTutoring, including at least $2,000 each month, on top of the 2% Advertising Fund. CoOp fees average about $2,000 per month and are credited toward the advertising minimum. Call Center $415 per month plus $34.47 to $76.50 per academic evaluation, required for the whole term. Conference Services $315 per month, credited against usage charges, required. Marketing Communication Program spend of at least $100 per month. Required accounting and payroll software from the designated vendor costs $125 to $165 per month.

## Fee notes
Standard first-franchise fee $49,000; additional franchise or Center Director discount $26,500; transferee fee $7,000 (may be waived); veteran/educator discount 25% off first-center fee; development fee $75,500-$129,000 for 2-5 centers credited toward franchise fees. In 2025, fees paid ranged $0-$49,000. Royalty is 10% of monthly Gross Receipts; beginning in the 24th month of operation it is the greater of 10% or $1,500 per month. A Base Royalty of $650 per month is also payable, beginning 6 months after signing. Monthly Marketing Fee is $250 plus 2% of monthly Gross Receipts. A Digital and Local Marketing Fee of at least $1,000 per month is paid to the franchisor and credited against the local marketing requirement.
