## Fee notes
Single fee of $49,900; waived for approved conversions. Existing franchisees pay $35,000 for 2nd+ stores. Employees pay $10,000. VetFran 20% discount; First Responder 10% discount. Multi-Unit Fee: $49,900 for first store + $35,000 per additional store. Royalty is 2% of monthly Gross Sales for the first 12 months of operation and 3% after. Advertising in the first 12 months of operation is a fixed $4,350 per month: $3,350 Local Marketing Spend plus $1,000 to the National Advertising Fund. After the first 12 months, the advertising contribution is the lesser of 3.5% of Gross Sales or $8,333 per month, with 2/3 directed to Local Marketing Spend (remitted to a third-party vendor) and 1/3 to the National Advertising Fund. Advertising contributions are capped at $100,000 per store per year. Technology Fee is estimated at $925–$1,200 per month based on store build-out. Point of Sale equipment must be leased from the franchisor at an estimated $265–$340 per month.
