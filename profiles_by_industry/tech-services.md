## Fee notes
First Franchise Agreement requires a $40,000 Initial Franchise Fee plus a $12,500 Initial Training Fee (both non-refundable). Second or subsequent Franchise Agreements carry a reduced Initial Franchise Fee of $25,000, with the Initial Training Fee potentially waived. The Initial Franchise Fee and/or Initial Training Fee may be waived for affiliate-executed agreements, conversions from independent repair stores, or purchases of existing affiliate-owned stores. Under an Area Development Agreement, the first store's Initial Franchise Fee is $40,000 and subsequent stores are $25,000 (with a $12,500-per-store Development Fee credited against subsequent fees). Low set to $25,000 (subsequent/reduced fee) and high set to $40,000 (first store standard fee), excluding the Initial Training Fee of $12,500 which is a separate required fee. Continuing Royalty is 7% of Non-Recommerce Revenue and 4% of Recommerce Revenue (Gross Sales from pre-owned mobile and other electronic devices), paid monthly. Technology and Customer Support Fee is 1% of Gross Sales, paid monthly with the royalty. Advertising Fee is up to 2% of Gross Sales once an Advertising Fund is established; as of December 31, 2025, franchisees were not required to contribute. A National Account Administrative Fee of up to 5% of Gross Sales from National Account services may be charged in addition to the royalty.
