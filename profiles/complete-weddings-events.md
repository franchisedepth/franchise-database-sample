## Fee notes
Standard fee $50,000. Incentive program (by 3/31/2027): Option 1: $40,000 initial + 8% royalty; Option 2: $15,000 initial + 13% royalty until $800k cumulative receipts, then 8%. Failure to open triggers additional payments ($10k or $35k depending on option). Website, CRM and Technology Support Fee is currently $83 per week (capped at $300 per week) and Computer System Maintenance, Hosting and Licensing Fee is currently $55 per week, together $598 per month at 52/12; the Venue Website fee of $300 per year adds $25 per month. Participation in the National Sales Program is required: currently $100 per week (about $433 per month at 52/12) plus commissions of $65 per service sold, or 10% of the total booking if under $650; the weekly fee may rise to $250. Local advertising spend of 4% of Gross Receipts is required, and the Google Ads Program minimum of $87.50 per week ($37.50 of it a management fee) counts toward it.
