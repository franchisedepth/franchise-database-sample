## Fee notes
Standard fee $50,000; military veteran/active duty discount 25% ($37,500); last fiscal year collected $30,000-$50,000. Multiple unit packages: $50,000 first unit + $37,500 per additional unit. Royalty Fee is 45% of gross margin on temporary and temp-to-hire placement revenue and 15% of gross margin on permanent placement revenue, where gross margin is Total Revenue less Direct Costs. Conversion franchises pay 35% of gross margin on existing business for the first two years, then 45%. Minimum royalty, assessed quarterly: $12,000 in year 1 ($6,000 in each of quarters 3 and 4), $100,000 in year 2, $150,000 in year 3, $200,000 in year 4, $210,000 in year 5, $220,500 in year 6, $231,524 in year 7, $243,100 in year 8, $255,256 in year 9 and $268,016 in year 10. Technology Fee $1,500-$3,000 per month. Brand Development Fee of up to 1% of Total Revenue is reserved; the fund was not established as of September 30, 2025.
