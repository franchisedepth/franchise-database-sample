## Fee notes
Base $55k uniformly imposed. Discounts available: $2,500 Franchise Expo, $10,000 senior-level employee, $5,000 qualified veteran; territorial: $30,000 for second territory, $20,000 for third and subsequent. Optional Licensing Assistance Fee $3,500; Optional Enhanced Support Services Fee $4,500. System Technology Fee $299 per month. SwyftOps software is required; its monthly fee, paid directly to SwyftOps and based on scheduled hours or active clients, currently averages about $100 to $700+ per month. Local advertising: you must spend the greater of 2% of Gross Revenues or $1,000 per month, in addition to the National Marketing Fee. The franchisor may direct up to 4% of Gross Revenues to a Local Marketing Cooperative.
