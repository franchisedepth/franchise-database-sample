## Fee notes
The initial franchise fee is $45,000, payable in lump sum at signing of the Franchise Agreement, and is non-refundable. During fiscal year 2024, franchisees paid an initial franchise fee ranging from $0 to $35,000. For Area Development Agreements, the development fee is 50% of the aggregate initial franchise fees for all Centers in the development schedule, applied toward each Center's franchise fee at signing. Incentive programs may waive or reduce the fee or modify payment timing. Additional required fees at signing include a $3,000 Software Installation Fee, a $20,000 Grand Opening Contribution, FF&E Package ($29,497–$39,388), and Opening Inventory ($23,000–$35,000).
