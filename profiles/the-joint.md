## Fee notes
Standard $39,900. 2025 Sales Incentive: Franchise Agreements signed in 2025 (through December 31, 2025) received an additional 50% discount off the initial franchise fee ($19,950 instead of $39,900). Veterans $6K discount ($33,900). Multi-clinic $10K discount per additional unit ($29,900). DC Path to Ownership $19,900 discount ($20,000). Non-combinable except 2025 Incentive. Local Advertising Commitment: the franchisee must spend the greater of 5% of Gross Sales or $3,000 per month on local advertising, paid to third parties and in addition to the Brand Fund Fee; compliance is measured on a rolling 6-month basis, and a shortfall may be charged at 10% of the deficiency, paid into the Brand Fund.
