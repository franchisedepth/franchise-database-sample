## Fee notes
Single-unit Initial Franchise Fee is $49,500. Under a Multiple Unit Franchise Agreement, fees are $49,500 for the 1st store, $44,500 for the 2nd, $39,500 for the 3rd, and $34,500 for the 4th and subsequent stores. A $15,000 Multiple Unit Territory Fee per store is credited against future Initial Franchise Fees. A $10,000 discount is available for qualifying veterans, active/retired firefighters, police officers, EMT/paramedics, nurses, and teachers off the first store fee. Existing franchisees in good standing for 36+ months may open one additional store at a discounted Initial Franchise Fee of $15,000 (lowest possible value). All fees are non-refundable. Monthly technology fees total $1,098: Software Support $509, Omni-Channel Maintenance $200, Avalara Avatax $27, Payment Gateway $22, Salesforce.com CRM $80, Managed Security Services (PCI) $200 and RepairQ $60. The Minimum Store Promotion Requirement is a pro rata $20,000 in the first calendar year, then the greater of 4% of prior-year Net Revenues or $20,000 per year; a shortfall must be deposited with the franchisor. $20,000 is paid to the franchisor at store opening and applied to the Digital Marketing Contribution, which will not exceed the greater of 3% of Net Revenues or the Minimum Store Promotion Requirement.
