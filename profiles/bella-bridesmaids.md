## Fee notes
First location $50,000 (nonrefundable). Subsequent locations in protected territory $10,000. Outside territory 75% of then-current fee. $10,000 security deposit also required. Systemwide Marketing Fund fee set by the location's previous year's sales: $1,000 per year up to $300,000, $1,500 per year for $300,000–$400,000, $2,000 per year for $400,000–$600,000, and 0.5% of monthly gross revenue above $600,000, paid in 12 equal monthly instalments.
