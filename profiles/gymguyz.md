## Fee notes
Base fee for 30,000-household territory. Additional $1.50/household for territories exceeding 30,000 (capped at 15,000 additional households). Discounts: $5,000 off for 2nd/3rd franchises or honorably discharged veterans (non-stackable). Excludes $11,500–$31,500 initial marketing fund and $0–$2,500 launch fund fee. Technology fee shown totals the $50 per two-week Technology Fee for a single unit (about $108 per month), the $375 per month POS System Fee (Mindbody), the $125 per month Microsite Fee (not to exceed $200 per microsite) and the $35 per month Hiring Platform Fee (plus tax), all paid to the franchisor. Search Engine Optimization Fee is $318 per month, paid to the franchisor. Telemarketing Fee is $299 per month, paid to the franchisor. VOIP Fee is $69 per month per line, paid to the franchisor. Social Media Posting Platform fee is currently $35 to $50 per month, not to exceed $100. The GYMGUYZ Training Application ($90 per month) is optional.
